FAQ & Confidentiality

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my uploaded images or confidential tables stored on your server? ▼
No. TableSnap is engineered with browser-side Web Workers (Tesseract.js and SheetJS). Your images never leave your computer or phone. No analytics or server uploads take place.
How does TableSnap reconstruct columns instead of single text lines? ▼
We calculate the bounding-box horizontal coordinates of every recognized word, clustering X-positions to identify real column divisions across lines. You can also manually adjust or add columns as needed.
What images yield the highest accuracy? ▼
Clear, evenly lit images with black text on white backgrounds and straight horizontal lines produce the best results. Our built-in preprocessor automatically sharpens and optimizes contrast before OCR begins.
